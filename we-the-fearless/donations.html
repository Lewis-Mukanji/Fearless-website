Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer with your organization?

Reach Out: Contact our volunteer coordinator via our website or social media. Orientation: Attend an orientation session to learn about our mission and roles. Sign Up: Register for events that match your interest and availability. Join Missions: Participate in mission trips to serve communities in need. Charity Work: Help with ongoing charity projects like food banks and donation drives. Special Skills: If you have unique talents, let us know so we can utilize them! We look forward to welcoming you to our community! 🙏✨

Can I donate goods or supplies instead of money?

We warmly welcome donations of goods and supplies. You can help by purchasing event tickets for others, donating clothes, books, and Christian materials. Additionally, contributions like food items, hygiene products, and school supplies are greatly appreciated. Your generosity in any form is a blessing to our community and the people we serve. Thank you for your support! 🙏✨

How are donated funds used?

Donated funds are carefully and faithfully managed to support various initiatives. They are used for holding and managing events, buying food for the needy, purchasing tickets for those who can't afford them, providing clothes for the homeless and needy, and facilitating our Fearless Event missions. Your contributions help us make a significant impact in the community and beyond. Thank you for your generous support! 🙏✨
